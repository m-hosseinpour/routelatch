R16-H panel visibility during the real and fake runs (2026-11-27)

- Environment: DISPLAY=:0, Plasma 6.6.7 / X11; a full-screen
  "kscreenlocker_greet" window (1920x1200+0+0) was IsViewable.
- A bottom-strip root capture (1920x60 at y=1140) decoded with Pillow was
  uniformly black (stddev 0.0 on sampled rows); the panel was not visible.
- Consequence: no panel screenshot was kept and no pointer click was used.
  Icon evidence is the exact StatusNotifierItem IconPixmap; menu evidence is
  the dbusmenu GetLayout tree. The probe image was deleted; this note records
  the observation.
