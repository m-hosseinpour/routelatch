# R15-D2 — real deedles tray on KDE (gated evidence)

> **Dated evidence snapshot (2026-11-26).** This file records the authorized
> R15-D2 live run of the product tray child. It is not a general status claim.
> R15-A evidence (`menu-*`, `FINDINGS.md`) was not modified.

**Status: gated run passed.** A real `routelatch run` supervisor on an isolated
data-dir registered one temporary StatusNotifierItem; `GetLayout` and `Event`
drove mode, stop, process restart and Quit; radio state followed the mode;
restart only completed after the `ready` event; a killed tray generation was
restarted and re-registered; every process and item was removed at the end.
No desktop or host setting was read as a target or written, and no real desktop
proxy set/restore was invoked.

## Limits

- **No human visual inspection and no screenshot.** The menu was driven through
  D-Bus rather than opened with a pointer, so icon artwork, tooltip rendering
  and radio paint are confirmed at the D-Bus/property level only. (R15-A
  already captured Plasma menu screenshots for the same host.)
- **Wayland and GNOME were not tested**; this host is X11 Plasma.
- **Desktop proxy set/restore was not exercised** and no desktop setting was
  touched. Quit was the plain `Quit RouteLatch` row; restore/keep rows are
  controller-mapped but not part of this gated run.
- Only the core surface was exercised (header, Mode, stop, restart, Advanced
  presence, Last action label, Quit). Pool/rules/proposals/desktop/settings
  actions remain R15-E..G work; no such action was clicked.
- The temporary supervisor/engine/tray processes and tray items were removed at
  the end; the isolated data dir under `/tmp/opencode` was left for inspection.
- **Probe side effect:** the version check for this report ran
  `plasmashell --version` / `kwin_x11 --version` without `DISPLAY`; each Qt
  probe aborted (SIGABRT), produced a coredump under `/var/crash`, and a
  DrKonqi handler appeared briefly. They were not product or tray processes;
  the running session (`kwin_x11` PID 4349, `plasmashell` PID 4374, both from
  2026-11-25) stayed up, and the DrKonqi process/tray item was closed. The
  Plasma 6.6.7 / kwin 6.6.4 versions are therefore taken from the R15-A
  snapshot, not re-measured here.
